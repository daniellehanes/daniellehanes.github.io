CrikeNight: building a dog-training app from 0 to 1.

A personal passion project — a companion app for dog owners and handlers that makes everyday training more efficient and helps sharpen performance when it counts most: at competitive trials. This page is a living log of the build, from first spark to working product.

What it is
A training + trial-prep app for dog handlers
My role
Product, research, design & build
Stage
Discovery → early definition

Why I'm building it

Training a dog for competition is equal parts consistency, memory, and feedback — but most handlers track it in their heads, in scattered notes, or not at all. Progress is hard to see, plateaus are hard to diagnose, and the jump from the practice yard to the trial ring loses a lot in translation.

CrikeNight is my attempt to fix that: a simple, structured way to plan sessions, log reps, spot patterns, and walk into a trial prepared. It's also my own hands-on 0-to-1 playground — a chance to run the full product loop end-to-end, on something I genuinely care about.

The 0 → 1 journey

How the product is taking shape, stage by stage. I'll keep this updated as each phase moves forward.

STAGE 00

The spark Done

The founding insight: handlers lack a single place to plan training, capture what actually happened, and carry that context into trials. Framed the core problem and the person I'm building for.

  • Wrote a one-line problem statement and a "who is this for" sketch.
  • Captured my own training pain points as the first source of truth.
STAGE 01

Problem discovery In progress

Talking to other handlers and trainers to pressure-test the problem before building anything. Separating what would be nice from what's genuinely painful.

  • Informal interviews with handlers across experience levels.
  • Mapping the current training-to-trial workflow and where it breaks.
  • Collecting the language people actually use, to shape the product's voice.
STAGE 02

Defining the vision Up next

Turning discovery into a sharp point of view: the one job the first version must do well, the metrics that signal it's working, and everything intentionally left out.

  • Product principles and a tightly scoped v1 definition.
  • Success metrics for training consistency and trial readiness.
STAGE 03

First prototype (MVP) Up next

The smallest thing that delivers real value: plan a session, log reps, and see progress over time. Built to be used, not just demoed.

  • Core flows: session planner, rep log, progress view.
  • Clickable prototype, then a working build I can use daily.
STAGE 04

Testing at trials Up next

The real exam: does using CrikeNight in the weeks before a trial change how prepared a handler feels — and how they perform on the day?

  • Run the full loop on my own trial prep.
  • Put it in the hands of a few trusted handlers for honest feedback.
STAGE 05

Iterate & grow Up next

Fold what I learn back into the product, sharpen the experience, and decide what earns a place in the roadmap next.

  • Prioritize improvements by real usage, not assumptions.
  • Shape a lightweight roadmap for the next set of bets.

This is an evolving log — I'll update each stage as the work moves from "up next" to "in progress" to "done."
